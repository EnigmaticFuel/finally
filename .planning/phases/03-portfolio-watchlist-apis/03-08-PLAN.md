<objective>
Make the error taxonomy total at the service seam, then correct four narrow trade-path
defects that a green test suite currently hides.

Purpose: `api/errors.py:40` registers a handler for bare `ValueError`, which means every
`ValueError` raised anywhere in a request — including `pydantic_core.ValidationError`, a
`ValueError` subclass — becomes a 400 echoing `str(exc)` into a field the contract says is
shown to the user verbatim. Server defects are reported as client errors and pydantic
internals leak. The fix is to translate the two known plain-`ValueError` sources into the
taxonomy at the seam and then delete the blanket row, so `ValueError` means "bug" again.
The four corrections that ride along are all in `trading.py` and all have file:line
evidence in `03-REVIEW.md`: the snapshot values the traded ticker at a price the trade did
not fill at, the reported cash balance is not the stored one, the cash guard permits a
sub-half-cent overdraft that stores negative zero, and a share count can render in exponent
form inside a user-facing message.

Output: `trading.py` and `watchlist.py` raising `TradeError` where they raised bare
`ValueError`; `api/errors.py` down to three handlers; a `prices[ticker] = fill_price`
overlay; one cash figure computed at storage precision, compared before rounding and
reported after; a `_format_shares` helper; a new `tests/api/test_errors.py`; and one new
assertion per correction, each of which fails against today's code.
</objective>
