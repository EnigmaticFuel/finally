<objective>
Make the portfolio readable and resettable. `GET /api/portfolio` reports cash, live-valued
positions and a total; `GET /api/portfolio/history` serves bounded snapshot history with
`?limit=` and `?since=`; `POST /api/portfolio/reset` returns cash to `STARTING_CASH` with no
positions and one snapshot marking the step.

Purpose: this is the read half of PLAN.md section 8's portfolio surface and the whole of
PORT-14, which has no PLAN.md text at all - D-10 through D-13 are its entire specification.
Phase 4's header, positions table and P&L chart are all built against these three shapes, and
Phase 5's chart reads history through `?since=`.

Output: three service functions and one composed reset transaction in
`app/services/portfolio.py`; three routes on the router factory 03-01 published; a new
`tests/services/test_portfolio.py` proving the valuation rule and the reset, and route tests
appended to `tests/api/test_portfolio.py`.
</objective>
