<objective>
Make lifespan teardown exception-safe, so a snapshot task that died of something other than
cancellation cannot leave the market data source and its `simulator-loop` running after the app
has shut down.

Purpose: G-03. Today `task.cancel()` is a no-op on an already-failed task, awaiting that task
re-raises the stored exception straight past the `except asyncio.CancelledError` at
`main.py:58-62`, and line 63's `await source.stop()` never executes. The source outlives the app
that owns it. Per D-G04 the loop *dying* is correct and is mandated by PORT-12's own
raise-don't-swallow prohibition - the only defect is that the corpse blocks the rest of teardown.
This plan removes the blockage without making the failure any quieter; it makes it louder, at the
moment it happens rather than at shutdown.

Output: a lifespan whose cancel-reclaim-stop sequence runs from a `finally` block; a
`_log_if_failed` done-callback that reports a dead recorder at error level the instant it dies; a
corrected lifespan docstring; and two tests in `tests/test_main.py` - one driving the real
lifespan with a genuinely failing recorder, one proving the callback stays silent on a clean
shutdown.
</objective>
